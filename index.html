IV — Service Record
The Litany of Krieg “In life, war. In death, peace. In life, shame. In death, atonement.”

192-178-3301 was born at the end of the 41st Millenium in 998.M41 and completed his training in 007.M42. He has shown exceptional skills with ranged weaponry and was specialized in Long Range Support and a position further back from the front line to assist the sieging forces by picking off key targets. he has shown signs of self-preservation. It wasn’t cowardice, but simply him fighting to stay alive. When he was questioned about his unwillingness to die, he responded with “I need to kill as many enemies of the imperium, only then I can find peace in my death”.

His first Deployment in 007.M42 acting as long range support within the 987th Death Korps infantry Regiment in the siege campaign of a heretic hive city of Diez. The campaign was a phyrric imperial success after 2 years of sieging. His promotion for becoming a grenadier was denied due to him not fighting in the frontlines.

His second and last deployment in 009.M42 was in the World of Cordis, defending a Forge World from an Ork invasion. In his last mission, he was assigned to a Kill Team to take out an enemy HVT. 192-178-3301 came out as a soul survivor from this successful kill-team mission. Earning him the name "Johann von Jascheroff"
